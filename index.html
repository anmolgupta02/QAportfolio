04 / QA ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY

Build quality capability, not just a bigger suite of scripts.

  1. 01

    Automation is a system

    Frameworks, test data, execution, reporting, and ownership all need to work together.

  2. 02

    QA starts before execution

    Risk, planning, requirements, and coverage decisions set the foundation for useful testing.

  3. 03

    Tools should solve real workflow problems

    Internal QA tools matter when they remove ambiguity, improve coordination, or expose risk.

  4. 04

    AI augments judgement

    AI can accelerate engineering work, but review, validation, and quality decisions remain human responsibilities.

  5. 05

    Quality should be measurable

    Clear signals from coverage, execution, defects, and risk help teams learn and release with confidence.
